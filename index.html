Sign-Flip Parity

Pick any four numbers and stick + or − signs between them. Now flip any sign. The total changes — sometimes a lot — but the parity (even vs odd) stays locked. Try it: each flip shifts the total by exactly ±2 × something, and 2×anything is even, so parity can never escape.

All 8 sign combinations

With three flippable signs there are 2×2×2 = 8 possible expressions. Watch how every single one lands on the same parity — there are no exceptions.

The set of 8 totals always splits cleanly: all 8 even, or all 8 odd. The parity is decided once you pick your four numbers — whether a + b + c + d is even or odd. After that, signs can never change it.
